**47 stories • 6 continents • one day: Sunday, 22 November 2026.**

One image that sums up the world's news on 22 November 2026 — from the capture of cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro" in Mexico and Iran's strike on Bahrain to the ATP Finals title decider between Alcaraz and Zverev in Turin, the missile strike on Kryvyi Rih, and the Democrats retaking the US Congress.

## What the image shows

- **47 stories across 6 continents** — Europe 12, Middle East 6, Asia 5, Africa 12, Americas 5, Oceania 4, World 3
- **Day in Numbers** — 2 killed in Kryvyi Rih, 70+ Saudi strikes on Yemen, 146 safe on the Air Niugini jet, 5,300+ Ebola deaths in DRC, 152 homes lost in the Peru quake, Evanilson's 6-game scoring run
- **3 illustrated hero panels** (drawn in code, no photographs): Mexico, Bahrain, Turin
- **A world map** with 11 pins, a **By the Clock (UTC)** timeline, an **Also on** list, and a sources footer
- **Date Check**: every story happened, or was first reported, on 22 November 2026 (UTC) — or on 21 November as a headline story in the 22 November papers

## How it is made

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) draws the whole page with its own 5×7 bitmap font and its own PNG encoder, and writes one image to `/output/entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB). Two runs produce byte-identical output.
